# projects/

- **Holds:** active projects, each under `projects/<project>/`: its notes, plans and material.
- **Type:** `project` for the project's own note (`projects/<project>/<project>.md`), with `due` and `area`; other notes keep their own type.
- **Rules:** when a project is done or dropped, its folder moves to `archives/` and its `status` changes.
